# Dataset Schema and Business Rules

## Tables
- The `faculty` table contains a list of all professors, lecturers, and academic research staff.
- The `publications` table lists all research papers published by faculty members.

## Relationships
- The `author_id` in the `publications` table is a foreign key that links to the `faculty_id` in the `faculty` table.

## Key Concepts
- **Faculty vs. Professor**: The `faculty` table includes everyone. The `title` column specifies their job role (e.g., 'Professor', 'Associate Professor', 'Lecturer'). To find just professors, a query must filter where the `title` column contains the word 'Professor'.
- **Citation Count**: This refers to the number of times a publication has been cited by other research. A higher citation count generally indicates a more influential paper.
- **Department**: This column specifies the academic department a faculty member belongs to, such as 'Computer Science' or 'Physics'.
