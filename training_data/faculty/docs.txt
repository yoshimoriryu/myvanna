The 'faculty' table contains a list of all professors and academic staff.
The 'publications' table lists all research papers published by faculty members.
The 'author_id' in the publications table is a foreign key that links to the 'faculty_id' in the faculty table.
'Citation count' refers to the number of times a publication has been cited by other research.
A higher citation count generally indicates a more influential paper.
The 'department' column specifies the academic department a faculty member belongs to, e.g., 'Computer Science', 'Physics'.
